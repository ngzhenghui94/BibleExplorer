About Bible Adventure

A non-linear way to read scripture. Most reading plans march you straight through from Genesis to Revelation — and most people quit somewhere in Leviticus.

Bible Adventure lets you choose your own path. The constellation shows every chapter in the Bible. As you read, the dots light up. The app suggests where to go next, mixing genres so you're never stuck slogging through a single book.

Your progress lives in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
